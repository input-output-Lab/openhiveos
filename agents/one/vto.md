## Core Focus

**What we do:**
We leapfrog automation capacity through Agentic-First engineering — building software environments where AI agents operate autonomously on behalf of clients, not UIs where humans operate manually.

**Our niche:**
We build Invisible Factories: self-managing software systems where one technical human oversees dozens of autonomous agents running dozens of simultaneous client projects.

**What we do not do:**
We do not build software that requires a human to click through it to deliver value. We do not build for the novelty of AI. We build for the operational leverage AI creates.
